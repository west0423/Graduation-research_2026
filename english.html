about

On this site, you can learn about terms unique to the Japanese language—specifically
「ことわざ」 kotowaza,
「四字熟語」 yojijukugo,
「大和言葉」 Yamato kotoba,
「オノマトペ」 onomatopoeia
—in an experiential way, starting from a single kanji character and engaging your senses of sight and hearing. On each page, the illustrations evolve in response to the words presented, and clicking on them reveals the meaning of the term.


Creator Akari

【ことわざ kotowaza】

A short, traditional saying, widely known among the public, that often contains a moral lesson or a satirical observation.

【大和言葉 Yamato kotoba】

Words that are originally from Japanese and are not borrowed from Chinese or other languages.

【四字熟語 yojijukugo】

A phrase made up of four kanji characters, often used to express a specific meaning or idea.

【オノマトペ onomatopoeia】

Words that imitate sounds or describe how things look, feel, move, or behave.
